# YOU REMEMBER, DON'T YOU? — MVP

## Premise
A first-person psychological horror game in one bedroom. A woman claims to be
the protagonist's mother. The player examines clues and speaks to her.

## Core loop
1. Explore the bedroom.
2. Inspect the photograph, letter, and medication schedule.
3. Speak to the woman.
4. Notice how her responses change based on discovered clues.

## MVP boundaries
- One bedroom.
- One stationary placeholder character.
- Three inspectable clues.
- Basic first-person movement and interaction.
- Placeholder dialogue and a separate FastAPI fallback endpoint.

## Not included yet
Gemini calls, animations, pathfinding, branching endings, save/load, audio,
complex inventory, and additional rooms.
